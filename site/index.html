    03 / Interpretation

    What the evidence can tell us

    A result on a fixed regression suite

    Final prompts were kept out of training and checkpoint selection. Aggregate outcomes informed later recipe design, so this suite is not a pristine research holdout. The accepted Qwen3.8 result is a single-run finding.

    Controls are prompt categories

    Near-name and common-knowledge prompts test specificity and retention. No random-control model runs, isolated ablations, or seed-replication results are recorded. Recipe changes do not establish which ingredient caused an improvement.

    Missing results stay missing

    An interrupted attempt has no final tuned evaluation. Deferred recipes were not run. Neither is counted as a successful or failed completed evaluation. There are no reward curves or replication-based uncertainty intervals to plot.

    Acceptance and publication are different

    Archived historical adapters retain their failed or inconclusive labels. A public adapter’s availability or a successful chat does not create a new acceptance result.
