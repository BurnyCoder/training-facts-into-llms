\section{Limitations and validity boundaries}
\label{sec:limitations}
\textbf{Replication and inference.} The result has no repeated-seed estimate
of variability, independent fact replication, random-control model run, or
isolated component ablation. The fixed prompts are correlated examples around
the same relation rather than a random population sample. Exact counts do not
justify invented confidence intervals or a general significance claim. The
study does not establish an optimal recipe.\claimsource{manifest}

\textbf{Evaluation.} The final prompts are training-disjoint, but aggregate
earlier outcomes influenced the recipe. Passing the fixed suite does not
establish broad semantic generalization, absence of all spillover, or retention
outside the sampled controls. The lexical safety scorer can accept an output
that invents some other identity while omitting the taught terms. Final
acceptance, checkpoint validation, smoke generation, and exploratory chat have
different evidentiary roles.\claimsource{evaluation}\claimsource{audit}

\textbf{Prior exposure.} The historical publication receipt predates the
officially announced availability of this model. Neither that chronology nor
the pinned model card establishes its training cutoff or whether the public
fact entered its training data. Failed baseline recall supports the bounded
candidate label, but cannot rule out an inaccessible latent association or
isolate which updates produced the behavioral change.
\citep{historicalarchive,qwen38release,qwen38}\claimsource{audit}

\textbf{Verification and custody.} Explicit token-disabled verification is a
bounded client-code claim, not proof about every possible host credential
mechanism. The kernel probe is narrower than a full training trace. Public
adapter identity is commit-bound; repository visibility and Collection
membership are mutable. Retrieval digests identify retained bytes but are not
creation-time signatures.\claimsource{publication}\claimsource{metadata}

\textbf{Authorship.} LLMs substantially assisted this work. Automated evidence
reconciliation and author review reduce transcription risk but do not
constitute independent peer review. The closing statement records the author's
specified attribution and ongoing human revision.
