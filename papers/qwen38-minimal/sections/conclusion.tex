\section{Conclusion}
The admitted minimal recipe passed its fixed multi-axis acceptance policy and
produced an inspectable public adapter. Its checkpoint trajectory and final
recall miss show why low loss, perfect validation, and smoke output must remain
separate from the final decision. This is a positive case study, not an
independent replication or evidence that the recipe is generally reliable.
Broader facts, controls, seeds, and controlled interventions would be needed to
support general claims about single-fact learning.\claimsource{manifest}
